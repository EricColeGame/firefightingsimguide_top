## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I earn every unlock using solo AI squadmates?
Yes. You can unlock the vast majority of trophies solo by issuing commands via the tactical wheel. However, online cooperative play makes coordinated tasks—such as simultaneous search-and-suppression runs—far easier to execute within three-star time limits.

### How do I avoid triggering backdrafts during rescue runs?
Always monitor door temperature before opening. Look for black smoke puffing under door frames, or check door surface heat using your thermal imaging camera. If the room is superheated, crack windows from the exterior or cut roof vents before opening interior doorways.

### What is the fastest method to grind total water volume?
Load a high-intensity commercial warehouse fire, establish a dual-line supply connection to a municipal hydrant, and continuously run high-pressure fog nozzles into persistent structural burn zones.

### Do custom mission settings disable progression in this firefighting sim achievement guide?
In most official scenarios, modifying core game files or using external trainers can disable achievement tracking. Stick to standard campaign difficulty presets and official map rotations to ensure your milestone progress tracks reliably.
