## Core On-Foot Movement and Interaction Layout

Operating inside a zero-visibility, smoke-filled structure requires precise footwork and spatial awareness. Your firefighter must navigate burning obstacles, crouch beneath thermal layers, and quickly interact with rescue victims or trapped doorways.

On keyboard and mouse setups, movement follows traditional first-person mechanics with specialized modifiers for tactical stances. Crouching is critical: dropping below the hot thermal layer reduces ambient smoke inhalation and keeps temperature meters within tolerable thresholds.

| Action | Primary PC Keybind | Gamepad (Xbox / DualSense) | Tactical Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Move Forward / Back / Strafe | W / S / A / D | Left Stick | Navigating tight residential corridors |
| Look / Aim | Mouse Movement | Right Stick | Targeting fire seats and structural weak points |
| Sprint | Left Shift | Left Stick Click (L3) | Rapid outdoor transit to hydrants |

| Primary Interaction (Open / Pick Up) | E | X / Square | Forcing entry, victim extraction, tool pickup |

| Flashlight / Helmet Light Toggle | F | D-Pad Up | Illuminating heavy smoke corridors |
| Thermal Imaging Camera (TIC) | T | D-Pad Right | Identifying structural hot spots and trapped occupants |

Player experience surveys highlight that toggling your flashlight and thermal camera quickly is vital when searching for structural victims. Rebinding the flashlight key closer to your movement cluster—such as a side mouse thumb button—drastically improves search speed during structural overhauls.

## Pro Tips for Configuring Controls and Reaction Speed

Fine-tuning your firefighting sim controls reduces input latency and eliminates costly errors during structure fires:

* **Separate Interact and Drop Bindings:** Avoid placing "Pick Up Tool" and "Drop Equipment" on shared or hold-to-activate keys. Setting them to distinct inputs prevents accidentally dropping charged hoses inside smoke-filled basements.
* **Calibrate Deadzones for Steering:** Emergency vehicles feature significant vehicle weight. Setting steering deadzones to 5–8% eliminates highway drifting while preserving tight maneuverability around corner hydrants.
* **Set Dedicated Keys for Thermal Imaging:** Never hide your Thermal Imaging Camera inside a deep inventory wheel. Keep it mapped to an accessible single button for immediate wall cavity checks.
* **Leverage Mouse DPI Switches:** Lowering your DPI when spraying charged streams prevents excessive hand fatigue and stabilizes stream tracking across large ceiling fires.
* **Practice Staged Entries:** Before breaching exterior doors, pre-aim your nozzle and verify squad positions. Fluid control execution prevents sudden backdrafts from overwhelming your attack team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I fully remap my firefighting sim controls on PC?
Yes, PC versions allow comprehensive key rebinding across movement, vehicle operations, and tool management. Players can configure primary and secondary bindings to accommodate specialized peripherals, flight sticks for aerial ladder operation, or multi-button gaming mice.

### Why is my hose stream difficult to aim with a controller?
Charged attack lines simulate heavy nozzle reaction force, which applies artificial resistance to player camera movement. Adjusting aiming sensitivity curves in the options menu or switching your nozzle to a focused straight stream helps stabilize spray control on gamepads.

### How do I issue quick commands to AI crew without opening the radial menu?
You can issue rapid contextual orders by looking directly at an object (such as a locked door, fire seat, or downed victim) and tapping your primary command button. Your nearest free crew member will acknowledge and execute the tactical task without interrupting your movement.

### What are the best firefighting sim controls for aerial ladder operations?
For precision ladder placement, using dual analogue sticks or dedicated keyboard clusters (I/K for elevation, J/L for rotation, and U/O for ladder extension) provides the smoothest control. This prevents sudden jerky movements that miss second-story window sills during rescue operations.
